`installer-welcome-cloud-v1.png` is an original illustration generated with the
built-in imagegen tool on 2026-10-15 for Mira Media Downloader's welcome panel.
The full PNG is retained in this source project; Inno Setup scales it for DPI.
Version 1.4.2 also embeds the same unmodified PNG in the native startup launcher;
GDI+ draws a focused source rectangle at the display DPI in both UI themes.
`installer-small-mira.png` is a PNG export of the project's existing `mira.ico`.

Final generation prompt:

> Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: portrait illustration for the left welcome panel of a small Windows app installer named Mira Media Downloader. Primary request: a cute, quietly charming illustration that makes saving beloved images feel cozy. A rounded friendly little cloud with a tiny smiling face gently gathering a few small illustrated landscape photo cards into a soft folder, with two tiny star glints. Elegant restrained hand-painted editorial illustration, soft matte gouache-like texture and clean shapes, generous breathing room. Warm ivory backdrop, subtle lavender, dusty blue and soft peach accents, charcoal small details. Portrait composition; the whole illustration comfortably inside a tall narrow column; modest subject around the middle with open space above and below. Warm and tender, polished and minimal, suitable for an adult desktop utility. No text, no typography, no letters, no logos, no watermark, no product UI, no screenshots, no high-contrast hard outline. Create a portrait image suitable for a 2:5 installer side panel.
